Note values to use

6/8 is felt in two beats of three eighth notes, so its values are the dotted half (a full bar), dotted quarter (one beat), quarter, eighth and 16th. Triplets are a 4/4 rhythm. The tempo is the beat: a quarter note in 4/4, a dotted quarter in 6/8.
